# Inclusive Tourist-Local Skill Matching Architecture for Bagalkote

## Core Objective
The primary theme of the Bagalkote AI Tourism Ecosystem is **Inclusive Growth**. Rather than restricting tourism benefits to high-end hotels or distant tour operators, the system directly connects incoming tourists with local artisans, weavers, home-chefs, local guides, and community elders.

## The 5-Node Relational Skill Graph
Every interactive experience in the ecosystem is structured around a verified 5-node relationship:

```
[TOURIST INTEREST]
       ↕
  [LOCAL SKILL]
       ↕
[LOCAL PROVIDER]
       ↕
   [LOCATION]
       ↕
  [EXPERIENCE]
```

## Concrete Implementation Mappings

### 1. Sustainable Textiles & Handlooms
- **Tourist Interest**: Eco-fashion, handloom textiles, natural dyeing.
- **Local Skill**: Pit-loom weaving with the ancient *Kondi* loop-jointing technique.
- **Local Provider**: Ilkal Saree Weavers' Co-operative Production Society.
- **Location**: Ilkal, Bagalkote District.
- **Curated Experience**: *Ilkal Saree Handloom Weaving & Dyeing Trail* (3 hours).

### 2. Geometric Textile Design
- **Tourist Interest**: Traditional craft design and fabric structure.
- **Local Skill**: Dobby mechanism setup for auspicious motifs (*Choukadi*, *Tulasi Katti*).
- **Local Provider**: Guledagudda Khana Weavers Co-operative Society Ltd.
- **Location**: Guledagudda, Bagalkote District.
- **Curated Experience**: *Guledagudda Khana Dobby Weaving Workshop* (2.5 hours).

### 3. Traditional Needlecraft
- **Tourist Interest**: Embroidery, folk art, women empowerment.
- **Local Skill**: Reversible counted-thread Kasuti embroidery (Gavanti, Murgi stitches).
- **Local Provider**: Bagalkote Rural Women Kasuti Embroidery Self-Help Collective.
- **Location**: Badami & Hunagund Rural Clusters.
- **Curated Experience**: *Interactive Hands-on Kasuti Embroidery Masterclass*.

### 4. Millet Gastronomy & Regional Culinary Arts
- **Tourist Interest**: Authentic regional food, vegan/millet cooking.
- **Local Skill**: Hand-patting Sorghum Jolada Rotti and stone-ground peanut chutney making.
- **Local Provider**: Local Women Culinary Self-Help Groups / Khanavalis.
- **Location**: Badami & Pattadakal Surroundings.
- **Curated Experience**: *Traditional Jolada Rotti & North Karnataka Culinary Experience* (2 hours).

### 5. Epigraphy and Rock-Art History
- **Tourist Interest**: Archaeology, Sanskrit/Kannada inscriptions, iconography.
- **Local Skill**: Epigraphical decipherment and Badami Chalukyan art interpretation.
- **Local Provider**: ASI / Karnataka Tourism Approved Monument Guides Association.
- **Location**: Badami, Pattadakal, and Aihole.
- **Curated Experience**: *Badami Cliff & Agastya Lake Dawn Heritage Walk* (2.5 hours).
