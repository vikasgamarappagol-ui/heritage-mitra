# Kudala Sangama and Almatti Dam: Spiritual Confluence and Modern Ecotourism

## Kudala Sangama: Sacred Confluence and Sharana Philosophy
Kudala Sangama is the pre-eminent spiritual center of the Lingayat community, situated at the sacred confluence of the **Krishna** and **Malaprabha** rivers in Hunagund taluk.

### The Resting Place of Jagadjyothi Basaveshwara
- **Historical Importance**: It is the **Aikya Mantapa** (holy resting place) of the 12th-century social revolutionary, statesman, and poet-philosopher **Jagadjyothi Basaveshwara** (Basavanna), who attained samadhi here in 1167 CE.
- **Basava Philosophy**: Basavanna advocated *Kayaka* (work is worship), *Dasoha* (unconditional sharing of food and resources), and radical social equality, rejecting caste discrimination, gender oppression, and animal sacrifice.
- **Aikya Mantapa Engineering**: Following the creation of the Almatti Dam reservoir, the sacred Aikya Mantapa was protected by an innovative, colossal cylindrical concrete ring well. Visitors descend into the dry sanctum surrounded by the deep waters of the river.
- **Sangameshwara Temple**: A 12th-century Kalyana Chalukyan stone temple standing at the confluence, featuring carved pillars and stone ceilings.
- **Universal Dasoha**: The Kudala Sangama Development Board serves free, nutritious vegetarian meals (*Maha Dasoha*) to all visitors every day.

## Almatti Dam: Modern Engineering & Ecotourism
Located on the Krishna River at the northern boundary of Bagalkote district, Almatti Dam forms the expansive **Lal Bahadur Shastri Sagar** reservoir.

### Gardens and Recreational Attractions
- **Mughal Garden**: A formally laid out 40-acre landscape garden modeled on Mughal charbagh designs, featuring stepped cascades, terraced lawns, and colorful flower beds.
- **Rock Garden**: Sculpted stone hills and natural rock formations integrated with artistic depictions of traditional rural Karnataka life, folklore, and wildlife.
- **Musical Dancing Fountains**: An evening laser, light, and sound water fountain show set to classical and patriotic music.
- **Boating and Wetlands**: Boat rides on the backwaters and winter bird-watching opportunities for migratory waterfowl.
