## Tourist Safety Guidance (Women & Seniors)
- **No Guaranteed Routes**: No route or destination can be certified as 100% risk-free. Travelers should observe standard safety protocols.
- **Visiting Hours**: Tour ASI monuments strictly between sunrise and sunset; avoid isolated cliff paths after dark.
- **Wildlife Awareness**: Exercise caution regarding bonnet macaques at Badami Caves; avoid carrying visible food packets.
- **Climate & Hydration**: Drink ample fluids to prevent dehydration, particularly during hot dry months (March–June).
