# Accessibility and Mobility Guide for Bagalkote Tourism

## Destination-by-Destination Accessibility Matrix

### 1. Badami Cave Temples
- **Wheelchair Accessible**: No (only the entrance garden area is accessible).
- **Staircase Profile**: Over 200 steep, rock-cut red sandstone steps connect Caves 1 through 4. Handrails are available along primary flights but absent in some landing zones.
- **Elderly / Mobility Impaired Guidance**:
  - Ground-level garden and ticketing plaza are completely flat.
  - Cave 1 is closest to ground level (approx. 40 steps).
  - Upper caves (Caves 2, 3, 4) require significant physical exertion.
  - ASI provides a wheelchair for loan within the garden perimeter.

### 2. Pattadakal UNESCO World Heritage Complex
- **Wheelchair Accessible**: Yes.
- **Pathways & Terrain**: Smooth stone-flagged pathways and level lawns connect all 10 major temples.
- **Ramps**: Ramped entrance from the parking area through the ticketing pavilion into the monument lawns.
- **Toilets**: Modern, dedicated wheelchair-accessible toilet cubicle on-site.
- **Rest Areas**: Shaded park benches situated throughout the complex.

### 3. Aihole Durga Temple Complex
- **Wheelchair Accessible**: Partially.
- **Museum & Grounds**: Ramped access to the ASI Archaeological Museum and main garden.
- **Temple Plinths**: Temple sanctums feature 4 to 8 stone steps.

### 4. Kudala Sangama
- **Wheelchair Accessible**: Yes (Highly accessible).
- **Facilities**: Continuous ramps, stainless-steel handrails, elevators to subterranean river passages, and free wheelchair fleet.
- **Buggy Service**: Battery-operated campus vehicles available for elderly visitors and persons with disabilities.

### 5. Almatti Dam Gardens
- **Wheelchair Accessible**: Yes.
- **Facilities**: Smooth asphalt promenades, ramped turnstiles, and golf-cart shuttle services.

## Universal Principles for Accessible Tourism
- Where accessibility data is incomplete, systems must explicitly label status as `ACCESSIBILITY_STATUS = "NOT_VERIFIED"`.
- Never assume accessibility based solely on photographs.
