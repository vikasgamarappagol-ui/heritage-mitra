# Festivals and Cultural Events of Bagalkote

## 1. Chalukya Utsava (February)
- **Host Locations**: Badami, Pattadakal, and Aihole.
- **Overview**: The official state cultural festival celebrating the legacy of the Chalukya Empire. Over three days, world-renowned and local artists present classical music, Bharatanatyam, Kathak, and folk dances against the illuminated backdrop of Badami's rock caves and Agastya Lake.
- **Inclusive Dimensions**: Includes a mega handloom exhibition, sports competitions, traditional wrestling, and folk street performances (Dollu Kunitha, Veeragase, Jagghalige).

## 2. Pattadakallu Dance Festival (January)
- **Host Location**: Pattadakal UNESCO Monument Complex.
- **Overview**: Organized annually by the Department of Kannada and Culture. Premier classical dancers from across India perform amidst the 8th-century illuminated temples of Virupaksha and Mallikarjuna.

## 3. Banashankari Temple Jatre (January - February)
- **Host Location**: Cholachagudda near Badami.
- **Overview**: A month-long rural festival and trade fair held during the holy month of Pushya on Banada Hunnime. Draws over 300,000 rural pilgrims and visitors, featuring chariot processions, rural drama, and massive markets for handicrafts, textiles, and cattle.

## 4. Basava Jayanti at Kudala Sangama (April - May)
- **Host Location**: Kudala Sangama.
- **Overview**: Commemorates the birth anniversary of Jagadjyothi Basaveshwara with Vachana singing conferences, philosophical discourses, and mass community dining (Dasoha).

## 5. Ilkal Vijaya Mahantesha Jatre (August - September)
- **Host Location**: Ilkal town.
- **Overview**: The largest regional handloom congregation, combining spiritual festivities with a multi-day textile fair driving direct artisan sales.
